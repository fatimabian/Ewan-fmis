Privacy Notice

Farmer Management Information System · Effective July 20, 2026 · Scanning section added November 2, 2026

The Office for Agricultural Services of Rosario, Batangas uses FMIS to maintain farmer registration, agricultural production, farm-parcel, supporting-document, and service-request records needed to deliver municipal agricultural services.

Information collected

  • Identity, contact, demographic, livelihood, and RSBSA registration information.
  • Farm parcel, land-tenure, commodity, seasonal, and georeference information.
  • Uploaded identification and supporting documents.
  • Service requests, internal remarks, status updates, and system audit records.

Purpose and lawful use

Information is processed for farmer registration, record verification, agricultural planning, service delivery, reporting, duplicate prevention, security, and applicable government recordkeeping duties. It is not used for unrelated commercial marketing.

Who can access the data

Access is limited to authorized FMIS administrators and Office staff according to their assigned role. Farmers do not receive direct system accounts. Hosting, email, or authentication providers may process only information necessary to provide those services.

Scanning paper forms (Scan & Auto-Fill)

Staff may photograph a paper farmer form so that FMIS can read it and fill in a record for them to check. This happens only when the Office has switched the reading service on; otherwise forms are typed in by hand.

  • What leaves FMIS: the photo of the form, which can show a name, address, contact details, farm location and other personal information. It is sent over an encrypted connection to an outside artificial-intelligence reading service chosen by the Office, and to the company that runs the model it uses. No passwords and no other FMIS records are sent.
  • Why: only to read that form and suggest values. The Office does not use these photos for advertising or to train its own models. The outside service has its own terms for how it handles what it receives, and the Office cannot control them. Staff are told to scan only forms where the farmer has been informed of this.
  • Checked by a person: the reading can be wrong. Staff compare every value with the paper form and nothing is saved until they confirm it. Staff can accept or reject each value.
  • How long the photo is kept in FMIS: photos are stored privately, visible only to signed-in staff, and deleted as soon as the record is saved, the scan is discarded or replaced, or 24 hours pass without it being finished. Photos read for the Farmer Registration form are never stored.
  • What stays: an audit record of who scanned, confirmed and saved, which values were changed, and the values the reader suggested. The photo is not kept.
  • Limits: the Office reads at most 30 photos a day, which limits how much is sent out.

You may ask the Office not to have your form scanned. Staff can then encode it by hand.

Protection and retention

FMIS uses password hashing, role-based access, protected sessions, validation, audit logging, secure transport in production, and controlled backups. Records are retained according to the Office's approved retention policy and applicable Philippine law.

Your choices and rights

A data subject may request access to or correction of information, raise a privacy concern, or ask about the basis and retention of processing. Some official records may need to be retained where required by law or government service duties.

Contact

For privacy questions or record-correction requests, contact the Office for Agricultural Services, Municipality of Rosario, Batangas through its official municipal contact channel.
