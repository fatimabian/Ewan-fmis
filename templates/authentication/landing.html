WHAT THE SYSTEM OFFERS

One workspace for Rosario's agricultural records.

These are functions available inside FMIS. They organize the office's existing records and workflows without claiming services that are not configured in the system.

Farmer registry

RSBSA-aligned registration and updating

Maintain personal information, supporting documents, and Slip A changes.

Parcels and commodities

Farm parcel mapping and crop records

Record Rosario parcels, map locations, commodities, and Slip B updates.

Decision support

Reports, statistics, and seasonal guidance

Review maintained farmer and crop data through filters, charts, and exports.

Service requests

Track farmer requests from filing to completion

Record the requested service, priority, assigned staff, progress, and final status.

Record history

See what changed and who made the update

Keep previous and new values together with the responsible user, date, and reason.

Secure accounts

Only authorized personnel can open protected records

Administrators manage the system while staff handle farmer and agricultural records.

INSIDE FMIS

A practical workspace for everyday decisions.

The interface stays familiar and straightforward while connecting records that staff previously had to review separately.

01

Farmer records

Registration, personal updates, supporting documents, and update history.

Slip A changes retain old and new values so concerns can be reviewed clearly.
02

Parcels and crops

Rosario-only maps, terrain or satellite pins, parcel tenure, and commodities.

Slip B organizes each parcel and allows multiple crop or commodity entries.
03

Service coordination

Requests, priorities, assigned personnel, progress, and clear status tracking.

Staff can find open concerns quickly and retain a readable service history.
04

Reports and planning

Farmer statistics, crop trends, filtered exports, weather, and seasonal guidance.

Reports use maintained Rosario records and can be downloaded for office use.
